Installation Instructions • Account Setup • Strategic Combat Meta

1

Download the EU1 Private Server Client

Obtain the latest official APK package from our verified Discord release channel. This custom client connects directly to the EU1 European server infrastructure.

Safe & Independent: The EU1 client uses a distinct package identifier. Installing it will never overwrite or conflict with your official Google Play Whiteout Survival application. Both apps run side-by-side.
2

Android Auto Blocker & Permissions

On modern Android operating systems (Samsung One UI 6+, Android 14+), sideloaded APKs are restricted by default:

  • Go to Settings ➔ Security and Privacy.
  • Locate Auto Blocker and temporarily turn it OFF.
  • Tap the downloaded .apk file in your files folder or browser to install.
  • Once installed, you can freely turn Auto Blocker back on.
3

Account Creation & Character Bind

Upon launching EU1 for the first time:

  • Select Register Account at the start screen.
  • Set your Chief username and password (keep these saved safely).
  • Complete the initial tutorial to unlock full settlement functionality, alliance access, and the Gift Code redemption window.
4

PC Emulator Setup (Optional)

If you prefer playing on a computer, EU1 runs smoothly on 64-bit Android emulators:

  • Recommended: LDPlayer 9 or BlueStacks 5 (Pie 64-bit instance).
  • Simply drag and drop the .apk into your emulator desktop to start playing.

Bear Trap: The 4 Joiner Multiplier Rule

Bear damage multiplies massively when rally joiners deploy proper passive skills. In any Bear Trap rally, only the first skill of the first hero of each of the first four joiners applies to the whole rally!

  • Jessie (1st Hero): Up to +25% total squad damage dealt for every rally participant.
  • Jasser (1st Hero): Another +25% total squad damage at skill cap.
  • Seo-yoon (1st Hero): +20% raw attack buff to all troops in the march.
  • Philly / Ling (1st Hero): Additional damage and attack amplification.
Bear Rule: Never lead a join march with Gina, Patrick, or Sergey. Send Jessie, Jasser, or Seo-yoon as your lead joiner to buff the entire alliance rally!

State vs. State (SvS) Prep Roadmap

Hoarding materials outside of SvS is critical to hitting Phase 9 milestone crates without wasting speedups:

  • Day 1 (Chief & Hero Gear): Spend Hardened Alloy, Polishing Solutions, Design Plans, and Essence Stones.
  • Day 2 (Troop Training & Promotions): Prioritize promoting existing lower-tier units to T10/T11/T12 for optimal points per speedup minute.
  • Day 3 (Beast Sanctuary): Feed your Sanctuary pets, use Beast Marks, and upgrade Breakthrough levels.
  • Day 4 (Heroes & Widgets): Pop your accumulated Mythic Shards, Exclusive Weapon Widgets, and Gold Keys.
  • Day 5 (Fire Crystal Buildings): Complete Furnace sub-nodes, War Academy research, and construction sprints.
⚔️

PvP Combat Ratios: The 50/20/30 Doctrine

Infantry absorb all frontal damage. If your front line drops, your Lancers and Marksmen receive direct undefended damage and wipe out in seconds.

  • Open Field Battles: 50% Infantry / 20% Lancer / 30% Marksman.
  • Sunfire Castle Defense: 60% Infantry / 15% Lancer / 25% Marksman.
  • Bear Trap (Zero Incoming Damage): 10% Infantry / 20% Lancer / 70% Marksman.
