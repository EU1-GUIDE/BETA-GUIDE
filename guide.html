GETTING STARTED

EU1 BETA Client Download & Setup Instructions

STEP 1

Download WOS APK Client

Tap the button below to download the official EU1 BETA game client APK directly to your phone:

⬇️ Download EU1 BETA APK
STEP 2

Phone Security Settings (Auto Blocker)

On modern Android/Samsung devices, external installations are prevented by default. Before opening the installer:

⚠️ Required Setting Adjustment: Go to your device's Settings ➔ tap Security & Privacy (or Security) ➔ locate Auto Blocker and switch it OFF while installing the client.
STEP 3

Launch & Create Account

Once the APK finishes installing, open the game. At the login portal screen, tap Sign Up at the bottom left to create your account credentials and enter the server.

🍎 iOS Players: Apple iOS client support is currently in development and will be available soon!
