FAQ & SUPPORT DESK

Server Intel • Mod Support • Troubleshooting

Is my official Whiteout Survival account safe? ▼
Yes. The EU1 BETA private server runs as a completely isolated application package (separate APK) on your Android device. It uses its own sandbox save data and does not overwrite or interfere with your live retail account.
Will there be server progress wipes? ▼
Because this is an active beta testing realm, database rollbacks or seasonal wipes may occur prior to major version patches. Full advance notice will always be posted in the official announcements channel on Discord.
Why is Android Auto Blocker stopping installation? ▼
Samsung One UI and modern Android builds block third-party APKs downloaded outside Google Play by default. You must temporarily turn off "Auto Blocker" in your device's Security and Privacy settings to install the client.
How do I redeem active gift codes? ▼
Visit the Gift Code Depot from our portal home, tap any code to copy it, then head into the game: Tap your Chief Avatar → Settings → Gift Code → Paste and confirm.
