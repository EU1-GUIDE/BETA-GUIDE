FAQ & SUPPORT DESK

Server Intel • Mod Support • Troubleshooting

What is the EU1 Beta server? ▼
The EU1 Beta is a sandbox testing realm designed to evaluate game stability, graphics rendering, feature enhancements, and server load capacity under active player conditions. Because this is a testing environment, all accounts and data are subject to a wipe prior to the official server launch.
When will the Beta test end? ▼
The beta phase is currently targeted to conclude in early November. This timeline remains subject to adjustment depending on bug fixes, community feedback, and infrastructure milestones. Full advance notice will be posted in our official Discord announcements.
Can I play on Apple iOS devices? ▼
Apple iOS client support is actively in development and will be released in an upcoming update. In the meantime, iOS players can play on PC or Mac using Android emulators such as LDPlayer 9 or BlueStacks 5 (Pie 64-bit).
Will installing EU1 affect or overwrite my official game? ▼
No. The EU1 client operates under a completely separate Android package identifier. It runs as an independent application alongside your retail Whiteout Survival game, using isolated save data without modifying or interfering with your live Century Games progress.
Why does Android / Samsung Auto Blocker stop the APK? ▼
Newer Android updates (especially Samsung One UI 6+) enable "Auto Blocker" by default to prevent downloads from outside the Google Play Store. To install the EU1 APK, go to your phone's Settings ➔ Security & Privacy ➔ Auto Blocker and switch it OFF temporarily. You can turn it back on once installation finishes.
