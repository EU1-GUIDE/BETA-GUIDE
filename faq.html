🛡️ How do I bypass Samsung "Auto Blocker" to install?
▼

On newer Android devices (especially Samsung One UI 6+), the Auto Blocker security feature automatically blocks sideloaded APK downloads from outside the Google Play Store by default.

To allow installation:

  • Open phone Settings ➔ scroll down to Security and Privacy.
  • Tap on Auto Blocker.
  • Toggle it OFF temporarily (or allow installation from your web browser).
  • Tap the downloaded EU1 APK to install smoothly. You can safely turn Auto Blocker back on afterwards!
📱 Can I keep the official Whiteout Survival installed?
▼

Yes, absolutely. The EU1 Beta client utilizes a separate, independent package identifier from the official Google Play application.

Both games run as separate apps side-by-side on your home screen. Installing or playing on EU1 will never overwrite, conflict with, or affect your official Century Games account progress.

💻 Can I play on PC using an emulator?
▼

Yes! EU1 runs smoothly on modern Android emulators. We recommend:

  • LDPlayer 9 (Recommended): Best overall performance and low CPU/RAM usage.
  • BlueStacks 5 (Pie 64-bit instance): Very stable with crisp rendering.
  • MuMu Player 12: Excellent high-FPS support.

Simply drag and drop the downloaded .apk file directly into your emulator window to install.

⏳ Are there server wipes during the Beta test?
▼

EU1 is currently an active Beta test server where we balance custom drop rates, event progression, and high-tier features (such as T12 Exalted troops and FC10). While we strive for data persistence, scheduled wipes may occur prior to major version milestones.

Beta Tester Perks: Active participants during beta phases receive exclusive commemorative Discord roles and booster starter packs whenever fresh seasons launch!
🎁 How do I redeem Gift Codes?
▼

Visit the Gift Code Depot on the main portal to copy active codes with one tap. Then inside the game:

  • Tap your Chief Avatar in the top-left corner.
  • Tap Settings (gear icon) ➔ select Gift Code.
  • Paste the code and tap Redeem to receive instant Diamonds, Fire Crystals, and Speedups via in-game mail.
